# Subway Challenge: Beijing Edition

## Setting the Stage

Unfortunately for me (but probably fortunately for the subway workers), the Beijing Subway is not 24 hours, unlike the MTA. On most lines, the first trains depart from the termini at around 5:30 AM, and the last trains depart at around 11:30 PM. Once the last train has departed from a station, passengers will be cleared and the station will be closed for the night. This means that I will have a hard time bound of approximately 18 hours (approximately because the last train *arrives* after 11:30 PM. On longer lines, some stations may not close until after midnight), excluding extreme tactics like hiding in the bathroom through the night.
